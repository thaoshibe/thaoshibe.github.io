We present uncurated retrieval results for 1,000 randomly selected test images. For each query, we retrieve the top 10 most similar images using: (i) Relational-based: relsim (ours) (ii) Attribute-based: DINO and CLIP

All images are sourced from the publicly available LAION dataset and displayed via their original URLs, which may no longer be active. We do not claim ownership and credit the original creators.
