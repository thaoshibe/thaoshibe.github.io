"I hope everyone can have their very own (multimodal) personal AI assistant, very soon."
To be helpful and relevant, such assistants must:
    . adapt seamlessly to user's inputs/ intentions [NeurIPS'23, CVPR'24, CVPR'26];
    . understand users' knowledge/ environments [NeurIPS'24, CVPR'25];
    . and maintain a (long-term) memory to provide more personalized and context-aware responses [camroll (arXiv'26), visualmem (arXiv'26), and... tbd ;)].
We have many powerful general-purpose models today (fig, left), and my research steps toward adapting them into more personalized ones (fig, right) (.❛ ᴗ ❛.).
