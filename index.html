> I'm interested in building multimodal personal AI agents [prelim-slides, comic] <
Concretely, I study how current models can:
    . perceive and interpret visual inputs in human-like ways (e.g., visii, relsim);
    . understand users' concepts/ environments/ etc. (e.g., yo'llava, yo'chameleon);
    . and reason over user's (messy/ sparse) data to extract relevant and useful memories (e.g., camroll, visualmem, and... something-good-will-be-here-soon).
Today's models are powerful but general-purpose (fig, left); my research steps toward adapting them into personal (and agentic) ones (fig, right) (.❛ ᴗ ❛.).
